Before you differentiate anything, say what kind of function you are looking at. Is it a product? A quotient? A function of a function? Can a log law or a trigonometric identity make it simpler first? Naming the structure is most of the work — the differentiating is the easy part. Each question carries three drawers: a hint, the full working, and the answer. Use them in that order, and only after you have had a real attempt yourself. Copying the working down is not the same as doing the question.
